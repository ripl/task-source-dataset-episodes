Thrixel benchmark · task sourcing · 12 Oct 2026

Ten random episodes from every real dataset

45 real-world datasets (human video and real robots; no simulators). For each one, 10 episodes were drawn at random from the released data files, not from teasers. Each clip is labelled with that episode's own task label. Short episodes play whole and sped up; long ones show a 10-second window. Clips play when scrolled into view.
